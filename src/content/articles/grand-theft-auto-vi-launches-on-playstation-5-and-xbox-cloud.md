## What happened

Grand Theft Auto VI launches on November 19, 2026 as a single-player experience. The title arrives on PlayStation 5 on Thursday, November 19, 2026, available at PlayStation Store.

Through Xbox Cloud Gaming, Grand Theft Auto VI is coming to both Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One. Players can access the title on day one via Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One starting at 1am ET.

## Setting and audio features

The newest entry in the Grand Theft Auto franchise takes place within Leonida, featuring the neon-lit streets of Vice City and surrounding areas. Six distinct regions comprise Leonida: Vice City, Grassrivers, the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, as well as Mount Kalaga. Character Jason grew up around grifters and crooks.

In Grand Theft Auto VI, players get access to extensive in-game radio stations offering Modern Hip-Hop, Classic Rock, Pop, Dance, Metal, Latin, Electronic, ‘80s, Blues, Talk Radio, as well as Ballads. A selection of downloadable podcasts is being added for Lucia and Jason to listen to outside of Leonida's traditional radio offerings. Players can enjoy radio broadcasts alongside podcasts while traveling in vehicles or walking on foot, using fresh earbuds and headphones linked to smartphones.

## Why gamers should care

Grand Theft Auto VI must be purchased separately. The game uses Xbox Series S graphics settings when cloud-streamed with Xbox Cloud Gaming.

Beginning November 11, 2026, users within eligible regions may purchase cloud streaming hours for compatible titles without needing Game Pass membership. Purchasing Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition prior to 23:59:59 provides The Vintage Vice City Pack and One Month of GTA+.

## Context and comparison

Grand Theft Auto VI is the most pre-ordered title of all time on the [Microsoft](/news/microsoft-announces-wolfenstein-3-and-updates) Store.

Additional Rockstar Games titles, including Grand Theft Auto V, Red Dead Redemption 2, and more, are planned to be added to Xbox Cloud Gaming in the coming months.
