## What happened

Path of Exile 2 leaves early access on December 11, 2026, with a 1.0 release that finishes the main campaign. Acts 5 and 6 arrive with 24 new areas, 18 new bosses and more than 70 points of interest, which makes the whole story playable from start to end. The launch comes after two years in early access.

## What is new in 1.0

Two classes join the game, the Shadow and the Duelist. The Shadow plays around stealth: monsters cannot see you until you get close, and stealth strengthens your daggers. Traps are set while hidden, go off on their own as you strike, and explode in the order they were thrown. Chaining kills resets the stealth cooldown, and the class can summon Shades and clones that fight beside you, with skills such as blade vortex and fire trap. It has two Ascendancies, the Assassin and the Infiltrator.

The Duelist swaps between sword stances, including blood and sand and ready stance, to boost sword skills like cyclone and hemorrhage. Its Knight Ascendancy brings a squire companion and further new abilities.

Grinding Gear Games is also adding 40 unique items and reworking more than 70 others, along with new skill gems, reworked support gems and a remade passive skill tree. The endgame gains more than 25 new maps. The Conquerors of Wraeclast league lets you team up with Varek and his mercenaries, choose the Karui, Ezomyte or Marraketh faction, and use a reforging crafting system, independent Atlas mission commanders and faction castle sieges.

## Why gamers should care

Version 1.0 launches on December 11, 2026 for PlayStation 5, Windows PC and Xbox Series X, and a Mac version is planned for the same day. The campaign becomes fully playable for the first time since early access began. Anyone waiting for the finished game, the new classes or the league has a firm date to plan around.
