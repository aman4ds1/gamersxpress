## What happened

Sony announced another [PlayStation](/news/grand-theft-auto-vi-launches-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-cloud) console exclusive adventure platformer where players play as a dog. The game is a third-person, story-driven adventure title set within a striking Flintlock Fantasy world. It is scheduled to release on PS5 in 2027.

Hounded is the debut title from IMPRINT Studios. IMPRINT Studios is an independent UK-based game studio founded by former developers from Sony, TT Games, Cloud Imperium Games, and SEGA.

Hounded has a confirmed release window of 2027 on PS5. The title is being developed using Unreal Engine, with a development duration of just over two years.

## Gameplay and features

In Hounded, players control Duke, a determined young Border Collie embarking on a dangerous journey through the charming countryside of Anglodia. Duke searches for his lost master in an epic journey across Anglodia, a conflict-torn world inspired by late 18th-century Britain.

Duke gains magical abilities from The Tide, an ancient, strange magic humming beneath the soil of Anglodia. Players can use the Power Bark ability to smash through obstacles in their way. The Power Howl ability lets players electrically charge mysterious technology found in the world. Duke can also look beyond the veil to discover time-bound phantom echoes called Imprints, re-experiencing events that happened hours before his arrival.

IMPRINT Studios developed a custom Motion Matching system for the main character over just over two years in Unreal Engine. The movement animation method animates all of Duke's movement by hand. This custom Motion Matching setup draws upon a collection of movements modeled after real-life examples, picks the ideal motion for each action, and seamlessly refines the entire transition.
