## What happened

The Outer Worlds 2 is receiving its first DLC expansion, titled Side Effects Guaranteed, on December 2, 2026. This release represents part one of a two-part narrative expansion. Part two of the story adventure will follow in spring 2027.

In Side Effects Guaranteed, a distress signal beckons you and your crew of the Incognito to a derelict ship. The resulting hunt for a cure leads to Elysium Valley, which is a brand-new region located on Eden.

## Expansion features

Side Effects Guaranteed introduces new enemies, gear, and flaws to The Outer Worlds 2. One of the newly added flaws is named "Must Have Been the Wind" after the meme.

The expansion raises the character level cap from 30 to 36. It also includes the Sub Rosa radio station, which brings 20 new songs to the game. In addition, a new game option allows players to start Side Effects Guaranteed directly with a pre-made character at level 27.

## Why gamers should care

An [update](/news/nier-automata-update-inflates-pc-file-size-to-366gb) arriving alongside Side Effects Guaranteed provides options to edit skill points per level up and lets players respec their character skills at any time.

The expansion releases on December 2, 2026. For players who bought the Premium Edition of The Outer Worlds 2, these narrative expansions are included at no extra cost. The content is also available through the Premium Edition Upgrade.
