## What happened

Nvidia announced the Rubin HPC GPU on November 16, 2026, at Supercomputing 2026. As part of the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform, this graphics processor speeds up simulation as well as data processing for analytical, engineering, and scientific tasks. Nvidia expects Rubin HPC to be available sometime in 2028.

The Rubin HPC GPU is meant to accelerate agentic workflows and agents used in science, engineering, and analytics tasks. It is aimed at scientists, engineers, and analysts working with large data sets. Nvidia said that the Rubin HPC GPU will offer a balanced design, scaling data movement and compute together.

Ian Buck stated that for agents tackling science and engineering, faster simulation means faster discovery. Buck noted that Rubin HPC quickens the simulations that agents rely on to evaluate ideas and direct future steps, allowing engineers and scientists to examine broader options and shorten the path to breakthroughs.

## Why gamers should care

Nvidia stated that the Rubin HPC GPU features a record number of CUDA cores, with more than 50,000 CUDA cores on a single GPU.

In addition to CUDA cores, the GPU includes sparse data acceleration capability. It also features seventh-generation NVIDIA NVLink interconnect technology, designated as NVLink 7. Rubin HPC is built to accelerate simulation and data processing across large data sets, with arrival planned for 2028.
