## What happened

Sony Santa Monica has delayed God of War Laufey, moving the PlayStation 5 exclusive by roughly three months from February 16, 2027, to May 20, 2027. Sony announced the delay in a brief social media post.

Santa Monica Studio confirmed it needs extra time for finishing touches. Sony stated that it will use the time to polish Faye's solo adventure, thanked fans for their patience, and promised more updates on November 4, 2026.

TechPowerUp News reports that a Faye-led game was first tipped back in April, before Sony officially revealed it with a 20-minute showcase at its June State of Play. Santa Monica Studio locked in the February date at San Diego Comic-Con in July.

## Why gamers should care

The delay shifts the PlayStation 5 launch of God of War Laufey to May 20, 2027. Eurogamer reports that the delay may provide breathing room for players with large backlogs. Eurogamer also reports that February 2027 will include titles such as the Fable reboot, Persona 4 Revival, and Metro 2039.

### Pricing

- **Standard Edition:** $69.99
- **Digital Deluxe Edition:** $79.99
- **Collector Box:** £129.99

Santa Monica Studio confirmed the $69.99 Standard Edition price and $79.99 Digital Deluxe Edition price. Sony put up the £129.99 Collector Box for sale with the old February 16 date attached.

## Gameplay and narrative details

Santa Monica Studio confirmed that God of War Laufey puts Faye, Kratos's late wife, in the lead. Sony confirmed that the game features an afterlife housing gods from multiple mythologies and brings back the original series' faster combat. According to Sony, Kratos will return in future games and appears briefly in Laufey, while the next Kratos game will supposedly connect to Laufey's events.

Polygon reports that the cast features Deborah Ann Woll as Laufey, Jack Quaid as Phranque, Perlina Lau as Rue, and Christopher Judge as Kratos. According to Polygon, the plot takes place concurrently with the 2018 soft reboot and its sequel, sending Faye to the Everywhen afterlife to attempt to help Kratos and Atreus from beyond the grave. Polygon also reports that Faye's combat is much more acrobatic and evokes Devil May Cry, including the ability to punch people so hard that their souls leave their bodies.

## Context and comparison

TechPowerUp News notes that God of War Ragnarök was pushed from 2021 to 2022.

Polygon reports that Sony Santa Monica has confirmed a new mainline game led by Kratos is in development. Polygon also reports that Amazon is developing a live-action adaptation of the 2018 soft reboot, with Dave Bautista set to star as Kratos.
