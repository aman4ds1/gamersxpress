## What happened

Microsoft confirmed on November 12, 2026, that the four-player cooperative PvE mode Firefight is being added to Halo: Campaign Evolved in a free [update](/news/nier-automata-update-inflates-pc-file-size-to-366gb) later in 2026. Attendees at Xbox FanFest on November 12, 2026, will be the first to play Firefight across new maps. Visitors attending Halo Fest in Seattle, WA, on December 12-13, 2026, will also get a chance to play the mode early. Additional details about the free update will be shared at Halo Fest, while Theater Mode was set to be added as part of the update.

## Why gamers should care

The Firefight mode supports up to four players in cooperative play as a free update. For multiplayer options, players can jump in solo, in 2-player split-screen co-op on consoles, or in up to 4-player online co-op with full cross-play and cross-progression support. The Premium Edition of the game includes the base game, the Alpha Halo Armory Pack, the Art of Halo: Campaign Evolved Digital Artbook, the Halo: Hungry Buzzards Digital Short Story, and a Digital Game Manual. The included Alpha Halo Armory Pack features 5 Master Chief armor skins and 6 weapon skins.

## Gameplay and campaign features

Halo: Campaign Evolved is a faithful yet modernized remake of the campaign from Halo: Combat Evolved. The story follows the Master Chief crash-landing on Halo, where he fights Covenant forces with AI companion Cortana to avert galaxy-wide annihilation. The remake includes high-definition visuals, updated cinematics, refined controls, and three new missions featuring the Master Chief and Sgt. Johnson. One of these missions is Operation: Meteorite, which features new environments, gameplay, and enemies. Players can also drive vehicles, hijack enemy craft, and pilot a fully drivable Wraith tank while utilizing gameplay-modifying Skulls.

## Context and comparison

Xbox launched Halo: Campaign Evolved earlier in 2026 at a launch price of $50 as a faithful yet modernized remake of Halo: Combat Evolved. The upcoming free update is likely to be the last bit of Halo content to be developed by what remains of Halo Studios.
