## What happened

Capcom announced a timed collaboration between Dragon's Dogma 2 and the manga and anime series Delicious in Dungeon. A trailer released by Capcom displayed the treats added in the crossover event. The collaboration launched on November 4, 2026 on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC.

## Recruitable Pawns and outfits

The crossover adds two free Official Pawns modeled after main characters Laios and elven mage Marcille. Players can recruit these Official Pawns by visiting Riftstones in towns and other locations. Laios retains his swordsman class using the Fighter Vocation, and Marcille uses the Sorcerer Vocation.

Players who load save data during the collaboration period receive free outfits based on the attire of Laios and Marcille. The crossover costumes automatically appear in in-game storage for use by both the Arisen and Pawns. Redeemed outfits remain available after the event ends.

## Why gamers should care

Players on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC can recruit the Official Pawns from November 4, 2026 until January 28, 2027. The update becomes available on Nintendo Switch 2 on November 11, 2026, with Switch 2 players able to claim crossover items on November 12, 2026. The Pawns remain available until January 28, 2027 across all platforms.

Players do not need the Dark Arisen edition to claim the collaboration rewards. The content is accessible by loading save data in either the base Dragon's Dogma 2 game or Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen.

## Context and comparison

Delicious in Dungeon creator Kui cited video game RPGs such as Wizardry and Legend of Grimrock as inspiration for the series. The manga was adapted into an anime series produced by Trigger in 2024. Season 2 of Delicious in Dungeon is set to premiere in October 2027.
