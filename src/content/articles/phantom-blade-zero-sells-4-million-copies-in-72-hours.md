## What happened

S-GAME announced on November 2 through the game's official X account that Phantom Blade Zero had sold more than 4 million copies within 72 hours of its October 29 release. The studio said the total covers all platforms, and the game is available on PC and PlayStation 5. In its post, the team thanked players for their support and called the milestone a major step for the studio.

The game is a wuxia action RPG and is the studio's first big-budget release for consoles and PC, according to TweakTown. Coverage of the launch also pointed to strong early player numbers and rave reviews.

## Why gamers should care

TweakTown reports that the game recorded 833,921 concurrent players on Steam at its peak, which it describes as a record-breaking Steam launch. The same report puts its 72-hour sales at about 40% of the 10 million copies that Black Myth: Wukong sold in its first three days in 2024.

## Context

Phantom Blade Zero is an action RPG from the Chinese studio S-GAME. The 4 million figure is the studio's own announcement. None of the outlets that reported it has published an independent sales count, so it should be read as the developer's figure.

Sales figures announced at launch do not show how many players are still playing a few weeks later, so the next picture of the game's audience will come from later reports.
