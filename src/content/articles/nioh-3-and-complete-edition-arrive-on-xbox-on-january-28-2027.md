## What happened

Team NINJA and Koei Tecmo are bringing the Nioh series to [Xbox](/news/grand-theft-auto-vi-launches-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-cloud) Series X|S on January 28, 2027, with Nioh 3 and Nioh 3: Complete Edition. This marks the first time the series has appeared on Xbox platforms. Team NINJA stated that the release celebrates 10 years of Nioh. Additionally, a listing by the Korean Games Rating board appeared for Nioh 3: Complete Edition.

## Why gamers should care

Nioh 3: Complete Edition arrives on January 28, 2027, for PlayStation 5, [PC](/news/nier-automata-update-inflates-pc-file-size-to-366gb), and Xbox Series X|S. The edition includes the full game alongside two DLC expansions. Purchasers of Nioh 3 on Xbox will also be able to play on PC with support for Xbox Play Anywhere. The game's second story expansion, Bloody Insurrection, launches on December 18, 2026.

## Gameplay and expansion features

Nioh 3 is a Dark Samurai Action RPG with an open-field format exploring feudal Japan. Players control Tokugawa Takechiyo, whose younger brother leads an army of Yokai to overthrow the natural order. Takechiyo survives through the intervention of the Guardian Spirit Kusanagi, enabling time travel across key historical battlefields. No prior series knowledge is required.

Combat allows seamless switching between classic Samurai and new Ninja combat styles. Samurai combat relies on specific weapon stances for combos, whereas Ninja combat features faster movements, aerial attacks, and elusiveness. Takechiyo can wield swords, bows, gunpowder weapons, axes, spears, and fist weapons, with equipment providing stat increases. Combat also incorporates Transformed Arts, Ninjutsu, Onmyo Magic, and Summoning Seals.

The Bloody Insurrection expansion is set in Shimabara in 1638, featuring a conflict between rebel forces, alchemists, and yokai, along with returning characters like Tachibana Muneshige from the original Nioh. The expansion concludes Takechiyo's story and adds new Martial Arts, Transformed Arts, Ninjutsu, Onmyo Magic, and Summoning Seals. It introduces a difficulty mode called Nioh's Journey, unlocked after completing the Enlightened One's Journey third playthrough, which drops Ethereal equipment for the highest rarity items. It also adds an endgame mode called Battlefield of Carnage, where players climb floor battlefields, combine boss battle [effects](/news/the-outer-worlds-2-expansion-side-effects-guaranteed-arrives), and enhance special equipment instilled with traces of the Yokai Realm.

## Context and comparison

Koei Tecmo published Nioh 3 globally. The game originally released simultaneously on both PlayStation 5 and PC back in February of 2026. The preceding story expansion, Hell Rising, launched in August 2026. The upcoming Xbox launch brings both the base game and its DLC expansions together for Xbox players.
