## What happened

On November 12, 2026, Microsoft announced updates including Wolfenstein 3, Halo [Firefight](/news/halo-campaign-evolved-update-adds-firefight-mode-in-2026), and classic Xbox games on [PC](/news/nier-automata-update-inflates-pc-file-size-to-366gb). Wolfenstein 3 is in production at MachineGames. id Software has started work on its next game, directed by Hugo Martin and developed by teams in Texas and Frankfurt. id Software stated it will be some time before it shares more.

id Software co-director Marty Stratton stated id Software is working more closely with MachineGames than ever after Xbox laid off roughly half of id Software's studio earlier this year. Stratton stated the studio is doing great and that a deliberate effort kept id Software viable across game development and technology. Asha Sharma stated that a single person will on November 13, 2026 be able to wield capabilities that once required entire nations.

## Why gamers should care

Halo: Campaign Evolved receives a cooperative survival Firefight mode in a free Winter Update, with armor code GWT9-DNBT-7X9S-DJCX-8KH3 available. DOOM: The Dark Ages adds Slaughter Mode on November 16, 2026. Xbox Playback lets players check their history, including total time played, most-played game, top years, and longest streak.

Minecraft offers the free OG Green Character Creator item from November 10, 2026 at 10 AM PT until December 10, 2026 at 10 AM PT. Gears of War: E-Day adds the free Xbox Anniversary Weapon Skin Set in the shop on Tuesday, November 17. Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 releases four commemorative liveries, while Forza Horizon 6 adds an original Xbox-inspired livery for the 2025 GR GT cover car. Digital Xbox Series X|S pre-orders for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 view the Inner Circuit M4 Blueprint in translucent OG Green.

## Context and comparison

Xbox cut 1,600 jobs in the summer and launched September layoffs that merged Obsidian and Bethesda and put Activision in charge of Halo. Further Xbox layoffs are planned leading up to January 2027, and Microsoft offered a voluntary exit offer to over a quarter of its workforce.

The id Software layoffs heavily impacted its Frankfurt arm, including id-tech engine designers. MachineGames used a modified id-tech 7 engine, called Motor, for Indiana Jones and the Great Circle. Microsoft contradicted fears that id-tech was shelved, though further reports left the question open. id Software has concluded active development of Doom: The Dark Ages following its expansion release.
